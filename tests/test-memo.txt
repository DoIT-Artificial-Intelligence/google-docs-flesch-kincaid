Department Memo on Benefit Applications
It should be noted that applications were reviewed by the committee prior to the deadline. In order to facilitate the processing of claims, applicants are required to submit additional documentation. The new system was implemented on Jan. 5 by Dr. Smith, and it is extremely important that everyone utilizes it.
We really think the process is very simple. Basically, you just fill out the form and send it in. Approximately 300 applications are received each week.
Due to the fact that the majority of individuals who reside in the state are not cognizant of the numerous benefits available to them under current law, the department has endeavored to disseminate information through multiple channels, including mail, email, social media, and community events held throughout the year.
The form was quickly completed by the the applicant. Please contact our office at your earliest convenience if you have questions regarding your application. Thank you.
